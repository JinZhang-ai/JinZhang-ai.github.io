## Overview

**BRIDGE** (Branch-guided Residual Interventions through Dynamics-aware Geometric Editing) studies how to identify promising model-supported futures early in generation and minimally steer the residual stream toward them.

The project separates the problem into two stages:

- **Early Branch Identification (EBI):** identifies useful candidate continuations from early-layer representations.
- **Dynamics-Aware Geometric Editing (DGE):** computes a minimal, geometry-aware residual intervention and verifies its effect through closed-loop evaluation.

The broader goal is to make representation steering more selective, interpretable, and minimally invasive.
