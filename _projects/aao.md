## Overview

**Agentic Affordance Optimization (AAO)** investigates how website structure and information presentation influence an AI agent's ability to complete user tasks correctly.

The project studies agent-facing affordances in interactive environments, with an emphasis on end-to-end task completion, correct item and merchant selection, and website-side optimization for reliable agent execution.

The goal is to move beyond optimizing pages for human browsing alone and understand how digital environments can better support autonomous agents.
